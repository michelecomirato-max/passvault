# PassVault privacy policy

PassVault is a password manager for personal use. This page explains what PassVault does with your data.

## What PassVault stores

PassVault stores your data in an encrypted database, protected by a key that only you know. Without that key, the database cannot be read by anyone.

## Where the data goes

The database lives in a folder that you choose, on your own device or in your own cloud storage. PassVault reads and writes only that folder. PassVault has no servers of its own and never sends your data anywhere else.

## Google user data

If you connect PassVault to Google Drive, PassVault asks for permission to see, edit, create and delete files in your Google Drive. PassVault uses this permission only to read and write the files of the folder you choose for your database. PassVault does not read other files, does not share Google user data with anyone, does not sell it and does not use it for advertising. You can revoke the permission at any time from your Google account.

PassVault's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
